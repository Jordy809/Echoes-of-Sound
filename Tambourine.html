Tambourine

The tambourine is a percussion instrument whose lively rhythms have enriched music and dance traditions for thousands of years. Consisting of a circular frame fitted with small metal jingles, the instrument produces bright and energetic sounds that instantly capture attention.

Historically associated with celebration, ritual, and communal gatherings, the tambourine appeared throughout ancient Mediterranean and Middle Eastern cultures before spreading globally through various musical traditions.

Though simple in construction, the tambourine possesses an infectious energy that continues to enhance performances ranging from orchestral compositions to contemporary popular music.
