Early Harps

The harp is one of the oldest string instruments in history. Ancient versions appeared in Egypt and Mesopotamia over 5,000 years ago. Known for their graceful appearance and soothing sound, harps became symbols of royalty, spirituality, and artistic beauty.

Strings of Elegance

Early harps were smaller than modern concert harps and often featured wooden frames with strings made from animal gut or plant fibers. Musicians played them during royal ceremonies, religious rituals, and storytelling performances.

Ancient Egyptian artwork frequently shows harp players entertaining kings and nobles. Their music was associated with peace, harmony, and emotional expression.

Over centuries, the harp evolved into the large orchestral instrument recognized today, but its origins remain deeply tied to the earliest civilizations of the ancient world.
