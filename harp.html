Early Harps

Elegant and deeply expressive, the harp is one of the oldest string instruments in recorded history. Early forms of the harp appeared in ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia thousands of years ago, where they became closely associated with royalty, spirituality, and artistic refinement.

Crafted from wood and strung with fibers or animal gut, these early harps produced soft and resonant tones that accompanied ceremonies, storytelling, and moments of reflection. Ancient artwork frequently depicted harpists performing within royal courts, symbolizing peace, grace, and cultural sophistication.

Over centuries, the harp evolved into the majestic orchestral instrument recognized today. Yet even in its earliest forms, the instrument embodied a sense of beauty and emotional depth that continues to captivate audiences across the world.
